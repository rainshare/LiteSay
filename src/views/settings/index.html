Help

One click to understand the page.

  1. Open a web page

    Refresh open pages after installing or updating the extension.

  2. Click the pink translation button

    Translate using your saved languages and service, or click the extension icon in the browser toolbar.

  3. Change languages and services

    Open the floating panel to choose source and target languages and a service.

  4. Selection translation

    Select text with the mouse or keyboard, pause, then click the pink dot. Other triggers include an icon, automatic translation, or a modifier key. Copy results from the popup; press Escape or click outside to close.

  5. Hover on text

    Off by default. Hold Ctrl over foreign-language text to translate or restore one paragraph. You can change the trigger and action in settings. Simplified and Traditional Chinese are skipped.

  6. Restore page

    Click the pink button or green check again to remove translations, including while translation is running.

Text in the main document is supported. Forms, code, editable areas, and hidden content are skipped. Browser internal pages, extension stores, PDFs, image text, iframes, and Shadow DOM content are not supported yet.

Page, selection, and hover translation send the relevant text to your chosen service. Settings and translations are stored locally; successful translations are kept for up to 7 days and evicted when the cache is full. You can clear the cache manually. API keys are encrypted and never exposed to web pages.
